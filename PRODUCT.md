# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on GitHub Pages from `main` (repo is public; business material lives in a separate private repo).

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-size businesses in Mexico (Aguascalientes first), of any kind: the page must never read as single-niche (owner's decision 2026-09-30). Examples shown: restaurants, clinics, barbershops, real estate, gyms, stores, and "Otros". They mostly arrive on a phone, from a WhatsApp message, Facebook post or Google Maps prospecting by the founder.

## Product Purpose

DIVRO builds AIs and n8n automations by business area: sales, marketing, accounting and operations, from a simple flow to a full system. Brand line: "DIVRO · IAs para tu negocio: ventas, marketing, contabilidad y operación". Not a WhatsApp chatbot vendor (owner, 2026-09-30: Meta AI already covers that). Success for the page: the visitor understands the offer in seconds and opens a WhatsApp chat to ask for a free demo.

## Positioning

A local, one-person-led automation service that installs, maintains and adjusts the system monthly, in Spanish, with no forced contract. The founder builds real n8n workflows (lead follow-up for real estate is the lead demo).

## Operating Context

Sales happen over WhatsApp (524495458788). Pricing model: one-time installation plus monthly fee. Current proposed plans (owner may change): Arranque $3,500 + $990/mes; Negocio $7,500 + $1,990/mes; A la medida desde $15,000.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Setup takes 7 to 14 days; month-to-month, cancel anytime.
- Third-party licenses or APIs are billed apart; IVA extra if invoice required.
- Must keep all WhatsApp CTAs wired through `script.js` (`data-wa` attribute + `WHATSAPP_NUMBER`).

## Brand Commitments

Name: DIVRO (the founder's umbrella brand, which will also cover clothing and software). Voice: Mexican Spanish, direct, tuteo, no hype. No logo asset exists yet.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no customers, testimonials, case studies, metrics or photos. Do not invent any. Demonstration content (chat mockups, sample leads) must be labeled as examples.

## Product Principles

- The first action is always "talk on WhatsApp".
- Show the automation working instead of describing it.
- Honest promises only: speed of response, 24/7, setup time.
- Mobile first: most visitors are on a phone.
